Our story

We're Singapore Polytechnic students running Coastra as part of our BETA module. Our mission: turning waste into value through creative design, donation drives, and a commitment to a greener future.

Every coaster is half recycled materials, half resin. That's half, not all, and we'd rather tell you straight. Each one is poured by hand, so no two are identical.

  • Midas Touch

    Made using recycled materials to reduce waste.

  • Handmade

    Each coaster is handcrafted, making every piece unique.

  • Stylish & Durable

    Functional, elegant, and built to protect your surfaces.

FAQ

Can I customise my coaster?

Yes. You can add a colour or a name for +$1, so a customised coaster is $13. Tell us what you'd like when you fill in the pre-order form.

How much is delivery?

Self-collection at Singapore Polytechnic (near Dover MRT) is free. If you'd like it mailed, we send it via SingPost and you pay the postage.

How do I care for it?

Hand wash only, and keep hot pots off it.

Is each one identical?

No. Every coaster is poured by hand, so colours and flake placement vary slightly from piece to piece. Yours will look a little different from the photos.

Can I put hot drinks on it?

Keep hot pots off it.

How do I pay?

We'll confirm your total and payment details with you after you submit.
